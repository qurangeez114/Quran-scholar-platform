“O sister of Aaron! Your father was not an evil man, nor was your mother unchaste.”
What the commentators say

Scholar Summary

The commentators agree that Mary is being addressed by her people, but they preserve different explanations for the phrase “sister of Aaron.” The important difference is who “Aaron” refers to and what kind of relationship the phrase expresses.
