3. Method and limits

Text vs. interpretation: An explicit name in Genesis or in a commentator's explanation must never be presented as a word in the Qur'anic verse.

Attribution vs. endorsement: A commentator may quote earlier reports without accepting their content; this page summarizes the commentator's own conclusion only where the original passage supports it.

Fragment vs. reconstruction: Manuscript lacunae in 4Q225 are disclosed. It cannot independently prove the identity of the Qur'anic son, the son's explicit consent, or the form of the replacement animal.

Similarity vs. dependence: Textual parallels alone do not establish direct literary borrowing.
