About this feature. Islamic scholarship recognizes ten canonical reciters (qurra'), each transmitted through two chains of narration (riwayat) — twenty authentic readings in total, all considered valid. Differences are typically vowel marks, word forms, or minor phrasing — not changes in meaning. This page currently has verified data for 4 of the 20 transmissions (Ḥafṣ ʿan ʿĀṣim, Warsh ʿan Nāfiʿ, Qālūn ʿan Nāfiʿ, and Al-Dūrī ʿan Abī ʿAmr), plus word-level pronunciation notes for Shuʿbah ʿan ʿĀṣim (documented differences only, not yet a full alternate reading). The remaining transmissions will appear automatically as their data is added.
