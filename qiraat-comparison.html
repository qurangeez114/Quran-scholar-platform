See how a verse's wording differs across the authentic recitation traditions — with plain English explanations.
What you're seeing. The Quran was transmitted through ten canonical reciters, each through two chains — twenty valid readings total. Differences are usually vowel marks or minor word forms. This page shows 5 transmissions with full verse text: Ḥafṣ (baseline, most common worldwide), Warsh (North & West Africa), Qālūn (Libya/Tunisia), Al-Dūrī (Sudan/East Africa), and Shuʿbah (historical Kufa). Highlighted words differ from the Ḥafṣ baseline. Every reading is considered equally authentic.
