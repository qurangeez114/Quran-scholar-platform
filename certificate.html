About QuranHikma certificates

Certificates record completion of a QuranHikma online course. Credit value: To be provided by QuranHikma. Accreditation status: To be provided by QuranHikma. See the grading policy and transcript page.

Students can find their own certificate IDs on Transcript & Certificates after signing in.
