How to begin

  1. Open the secure invitation link in the email sent when your account was created.
  2. Sign in with the registered email and the temporary password supplied by your instructor separately. You may instead choose Forgot Password and set your own password using email verification.
  3. On your first sign-in, select your own password of at least 12 characters to protect your account.
  4. Open your Student Dashboard, choose the assigned course, and start learning.
