## Source-critical findings
- **Q 2:84 ↔ Exodus 24:7:** The Hebrew covenant book is read aloud and the people undertake to obey (JPS 1917 and Revised JPS 2023; [primary passage](https://www.sefaria.org/Exodus.24.7)). Q 2:84 specifically prohibits shedding one another's blood and expelling one another. Exodus 24:7 itself does **not** specify those commands. Label: loose covenant motif, **not** a direct narrative parallel.
- **Q 2:85 ↔ Jeremiah 7:9–10:** The Hebrew prophetic temple sermon criticizes murder and religious presumption ([primary passage](https://www.sefaria.org/Jeremiah.7.9-10)). Q 2:85 describes community killing, expulsion, ransom, and selective belief in scripture. Jeremiah does not narrate that episode. Label: ethical critique motif, **not** a common event.
