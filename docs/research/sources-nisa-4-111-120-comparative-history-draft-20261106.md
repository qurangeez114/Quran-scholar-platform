## Passage context

4:111–115 addresses culpability, false accusation, advice, and opposition to the messenger. 4:116–120 turns to associating others with God, polemical descriptions of invoked deities, and Satan's misleading promises. These are not a single retold biblical story. No specific external parallel has yet been established for 4:111–115 or 4:118–119; these preliminary negative findings are not exhaustive.

## Interpretive alternatives and research questions

The common language of exclusive worship and deceptive promises may reflect broad ancient Near Eastern and late-antique religious discourse; distinctive verbal or structural borrowing is not demonstrated. For 4:117, investigate the Arabic wording and securely dated evidence for female deity invocation; do not assign particular Arabian deities without evidence.
