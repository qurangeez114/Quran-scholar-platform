## Source-critical comparison: Qur'an 2:196 and Numbers 6:13–21

[Mechon-Mamre, Numbers 6](https://mechon-mamre.org/e/et/et0406.htm) describes a Nazirite's completion of a dedicated vow at the Tent of Meeting: offerings of lambs and a ram, shaving the consecrated head, and burning the hair beneath the peace offering (6:13–21). Qur'an 2:196 addresses completion of Hajj and Umrah, sacrifice, impediments, head-shaving, illness, fasting and charity. These share a **ritual motif** of offerings and hair removal, not the same rite, location, calendar, participants or sacrificial sequence. Do not infer borrowing.

**Textual chronology:** The Numbers tradition is part of the ancient Pentateuch, but literary formation, Hebrew textual stabilization, Septuagint translation, and surviving manuscript witnesses are separate questions. A modern English translation is not a pre-Islamic witness. Original-language and edition-specific manuscript comparison remains pending.
