## Specific source-text comparisons
1. **2:71 / Numbers 19:2–3:** Both describe a sound, unworked bovine. Numbers' Hebrew *parah adumah* means red heifer; Qur'an 2:69 says yellow (*safraʾ*). Numbers' ritual concerns purification from corpse impurity, whereas Qur'an 2:72–73 places the animal in a murder/revival narrative. [Hebrew and English Numbers 19](https://www.sefaria.org/Numbers.19.2-3). This is a qualified *ritual detail comparison*, not a demonstrated shared event.
2. **2:74 / Ezekiel 36:26:** Stone-heart imagery; Ezekiel promises transformation while the Qur'an reproaches hard hearts and elaborates rock-water imagery. [Ezekiel 36:26](https://www.sefaria.org/Ezekiel.36.26). Broad motif only.
3. **2:80 / Jeremiah 7:4–10:** Warning against misplaced religious confidence. Jeremiah addresses temple security, not a limited duration of punishment in fire; this is a *weak contrast*, not a specific parallel. [Jeremiah 7:4–10](https://www.sefaria.org/Jeremiah.7.4-10). May be unsuitable for Sources.
