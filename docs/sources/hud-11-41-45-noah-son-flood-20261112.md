## Textual, geographical and chronological cautions

Genesis 8:2 refers to fountains of the deep and floodgates of the heavens being stopped; Qur'an 11:44 commands earth to swallow its water and sky to cease. These are narratively related but not verbatim equivalents. The biblical Ararat and Qur'anic al-Judi must not be silently equated. Targum Onkelos Genesis 8:4 uses **Kardu** in an accessible Aramaic witness (https://www.sefaria.org/Onkelos_Genesis.8.4), but the edition, transmission and pre-Islamic attestation of this wording have **not** been established here; it is an unpublished investigation lead, not a verified pre-Islamic source.

The Hebrew Genesis account is part of a pre-Qur'anic textual tradition. Composition/redaction, extant manuscript witnesses and the modern online edition are distinct chronological categories. The Greek Septuagint, Syriac Peshitta, Dead Sea witnesses, early Jewish interpretation, and Mesopotamian flood literature were **not** collated for this pass. The existence of shared motifs cannot alone establish copying, a single intermediate source or direction of dependence.
