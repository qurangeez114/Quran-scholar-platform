# Moses, Pharaoh and the magicians — research dossier (unpublished draft)

**Status:** Working research notes, **not** a verified/public Stories or History dossier. Related Qur'an: 10:79–85; this batch reviews 10:81–85 only. Date screened: 2026-11-11.

## Primary-text comparison
Qur'an 10:81: Moses responds after the magicians cast, calls their display sorcery, and announces that God will invalidate it. See Arabic and English at https://quran.com/en/yunus/80-81 .

Exodus 8:18–19: Egyptian magicians cannot replicate the plague of gnats and say to Pharaoh that it is God's finger; Pharaoh does not listen. English translations at https://biblehub.com/exodus/8-18.htm and https://biblehub.com/exodus/8-19.htm .

**Shared element:** limits of Egyptian court magic in a Moses–Pharaoh narrative. **Important differences:** Exodus 8 places the magicians' failure during the gnats plague, with the magicians acknowledging divine power; Qur'an 10:81 has Moses directly condemn the magicians' prior display. Exodus 7:10–12, a different staff episode, may be a closer narrative setting but must be separately collated. These are not word-for-word parallels and do not demonstrate direct literary dependence.
