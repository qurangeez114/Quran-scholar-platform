## Historical dossier draft: marriage, menstruation and divorce

**Context and chronology.** Deuteronomy and Leviticus preserve pre-Islamic Israelite legal traditions; their compositional layers and dates are debated. Online Hebrew Masoretic text and modern translations are not themselves dated pre-Islamic manuscripts. Before publication compare textual witnesses including Greek Septuagint where relevant, and distinguish the date of a witness from the proposed origin of a rule. Mishnah Niddah and Gittin may illuminate late-antique interpretation but must not be silently equated with biblical wording or early Islam.
