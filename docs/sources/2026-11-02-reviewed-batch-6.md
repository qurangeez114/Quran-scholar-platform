## Reviewed and verified: Qur'an 19:57 — 1 Enoch 70:1–4

- **Source:** Anonymous *Book of Parables* (1 Enoch 37–71), specifically **1 Enoch 70:1–4**, especially vv. 1–3, in R. H. Charles's English edition. Primary translation: https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/The_Book_of_Enoch_(Charles)/Chapter_70 . This is a modern transcription of an edited translation, **not an ancient manuscript**.
- **Qur'anic locator:** Qur'an **19:57**, read with 19:56 which identifies Idris as a truthful prophet. The prior lead was keyed to 19:56; it is now keyed to **19:57**, where the elevation actually occurs.
- **Textual relationship:** **limited narrative motif parallel**. 1 Enoch 70:1–3 depicts Enoch elevated from the earth by chariots of the spirit. Qur'an 19:57 states that Idris was raised to a high station. The Qur'an does **not** name Enoch or mention chariots, winds, angels or a heavenly journey in this verse. Idris=Enoch is a later interpretive identification; this is **not proof of borrowing**.
- **Composition versus witnesses:** The date of the *Book of Parables* is debated, often assigned around the first century BCE/CE. No Parables fragments have been identified among the Qumran Enoch fragments. The work survives in later Ge'ez transmission, distinct from its proposed composition date and from Charles's 1912 English edition. Scholarship: Devorah Dimant (2005), *The Book of Parables (1 Enoch 37–71) and the Qumran Scrolls*, *Meghillot* 3:49–67, https://cris.haifa.ac.il/en/publications/%D7%A1%D7%A4%D7%A8-%D7%94%D7%9E%D7%A9%D7%9C%D7%99%D7%9D-%D7%97%D7%A0%D7%95%D7%9A-%D7%94%D7%97%D7%91%D7%A9%D7%99-%D7%9C%D7%96-%D7%A2%D7%90-%D7%95%D7%9E%D7%92%D7%99%D7%9C%D7%95%D7%AA-%D7%A7%D7%95%D7%9E%D7%A8%D7%90%D7%9F/ .
