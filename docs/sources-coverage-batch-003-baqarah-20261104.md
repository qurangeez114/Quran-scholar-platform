## Evidence candidates
- **2:14 ↔ Isaiah 29:13** — A broad parallel between externally professed religion and inward disloyalty. Isaiah describes honoring God with lips despite distant hearts; Qur'an 2:14 contrasts a group's professions before believers with its private conduct and mockery. This is **thematic**, not a claim of identical wording or direct dependence. [KJV Isaiah 29:13](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Isaiah+29%3A13&version=KJV).
- **2:18 ↔ Isaiah 6:9–10** — Hearing and sight as indicators of response to revelation. Isaiah speaks of a people who hear or see without understanding; Qur'an 2:18 presents deafness, muteness, and blindness within a parable. These have **different textual and historical contexts**. [KJV Isaiah 6:9–10](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Isaiah+6%3A9-10&version=KJV).

Both English source passages were inspected, but **the Hebrew text, critical editions, manuscript witnesses, historical interpretations and specificity threshold have not been sufficiently reviewed**. Both are staged candidates rather than published associations.
