## Primary source locations

**Arabic witness:** The Tanwīr al-Miqbās recension presented under al-Fīrūzābādī in the Quranpedia Arabic edition, https://quranpedia.net/surah/1/109/book/363, heading `آية رقم ٢`. Relevant Arabic explanation is the short phrase `من دون الله من الأوثان` attached to 109:2.

**Published English witness:** *Tanwīr al-Miqbās min Tafsīr Ibn ʿAbbās*, English translation credited to Mokrane Guezzou and hosted by the Royal Aal al-Bayt Institute / Altafsir in the single-volume PDF https://www.altafsir.com/Books/IbnAbbas.pdf. See **PDF page 786** (zero-based page index 785, printed footer page **774**), in the Surah 109 heading and the distinct `[109:2]` paragraph. English verse-2 gloss identifies the objects as idols worshipped besides Allah. This is **published tafsīr commentary**, not a translation of the Qurʾānic verse alone.

The verse-2 page screenshot was inspected visually, not inferred only from search snippets. The following verse 109:3 is separately labeled, so the verse-2 gloss is not a misplaced excerpt from a neighboring passage. Link to the publisher-hosted volume rather than redistributing it in the repository; observe the publisher's reproduction restrictions.

## Adjacent verse-level context to preserve

The published PDF also has distinct entries for 109:1–6. At 109:3, the translation applies a *future* sense; at 109:5, it applies a *past* sense. The Arabic witness explicitly applies futurity to a pair of clauses at verse 3 (`وهذان فى المستقبل`) and pastness to a pair at verse 5 (`وهذان في الماضى`). Those shared-clause scopes must be represented in W02 and W04 rather than narrowed to a single verse or counted as separate source voices. Full verse-by-verse English–Arabic fidelity for the remaining verses is **not** claimed here.
