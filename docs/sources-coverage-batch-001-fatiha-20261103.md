## Research limitations

These are **translation-checked research candidates**, not completed critical-edition verifications. Hebrew/Greek text alignment, redaction/manuscript dating, historical transmission plausibility, critical bibliographies and additional Jewish/Christian prayer witnesses remain to check. An earlier theological theme does not entail direct literary borrowing. Do not publish a Sources card merely because biblical and Qur'anic terms resemble one another.
