## Source-text comparison: 2:30 and Genesis 1:26–28

- [Qur'an 2:30, Quranic Arabic Corpus with multiple translations](https://corpus.quran.com/translation.jsp?chapter=2&verse=30)
- [Genesis 1:26–28, Sefaria Hebrew and JPS translation](https://www.sefaria.org/Genesis.1.26-28)
- [Genesis 1:26–28, Vatican Bible edition](https://www.vatican.va/archive/bible/genesis/documents/bible_genesis_en.html)

**Shared theme:** God creates or appoints humanity for an earthly role, stated in relation to a heavenly/divine decision. **Important differences:** Genesis grants humanity dominion over living creatures and refers to divine likeness, whereas Qur'an 2:30 describes a `khalifa` on earth and an explicit conversation with angels about anticipated violence and God's knowledge. The precise meaning of Genesis's first-person plural remains debated and the Hebrew passage does not explicitly identify angels. No direct verbatim quotation, shared angelic dialogue, or historical dependence is demonstrated.

**Status:** staged *specific narrative comparison candidate*, not verified/public; investigate Hebrew/Arabic semantics, earlier Jewish exegetical witnesses, composition versus surviving witnesses, and academic alternatives before publication.
