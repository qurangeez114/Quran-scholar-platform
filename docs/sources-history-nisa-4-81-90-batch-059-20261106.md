# An-Nisāʾ 4:81–90 — unpublished comparative history dossier

Date: 2026-11-06; batch 059. **Preliminary research, not a published verified dossier.**

## Narrative and historical setting
The passage addresses obedience and private dissent (4:81–82), transmission of alarming news (4:83), fighting and encouragement (4:84), mediation (4:85), greeting (4:86), resurrection (4:87), disputes about perceived hypocrites (4:88–89), and treaty/noncombatant exceptions (4:90). These are related community-regulation themes, not a single continuous event with a securely identifiable earlier textual prototype. The historical circumstances and classical asbāb al-nuzūl reports require separate source-critical examination.

## Scholarly interpretation and competing explanations
These two passages share a broad peace/war legal subject but differ materially. Independent responses to common ancient warfare problems are plausible; no borrowing, oral-transmission path, or direct dependence is established. The comparison may be too general to qualify as a per-ayah Sources card. The dossier should eventually include critical editions, historical scholarship and variant witnesses before editorial verification.
