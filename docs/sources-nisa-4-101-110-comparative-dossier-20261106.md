## Passage structure and historical questions
- **4:101–103:** travel prayer, congregational prayer under military threat, weapon readiness, remembrance and scheduled prayer. Investigate ancient Jewish and Christian ritual prayer in emergencies only where a specific dated text and locator are available. Do not conflate general armed vigilance with a ritual prayer prescription.
- **4:104–109:** perseverance, adjudication, dishonest advocacy, divine omniscience and forgiveness. The sequence is not a retelling of a known biblical narrative.
- **4:110:** wrongdoing, seeking forgiveness and divine mercy.

## Scholarly interpretation and alternatives
These are broad cross-tradition themes rather than distinctive shared narratives. One explanation is recurring ethical and practical problems faced by religious communities; another is a common late-antique scriptural discourse. Neither can be preferred as a transmission hypothesis on the present evidence. A rigorous future dossier should consult academic critical editions, textual witnesses, legal/ritual history and documented secondary literature; **no specific scholarly conclusion or bibliography is asserted without source review**.
