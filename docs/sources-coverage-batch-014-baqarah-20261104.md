## Primary-text comparisons
1. **Q 2:124 ↔ Genesis 17:1–14**, [Hebrew Masoretic and Revised JPS 2023](https://www.sefaria.org/Genesis.17.1-14). Both concern Abraham's divine covenant, but Genesis specifies circumcision and the land of Canaan, whereas Qur'an 2:124 emphasizes divine testing, Abraham's appointment as leader, and exclusion of wrongdoers. A shared Abrahamic covenant tradition is plausible; neither direct quotation nor historical dependence is demonstrated.
2. **Q 2:125 and 2:127 ↔ Genesis 12:7–8**, [Hebrew Masoretic and Revised JPS 2023](https://www.sefaria.org/Genesis.12.7-8). Both connect Abraham to a worship structure. Genesis describes altars in Canaan, near Shechem and Bethel, whereas the Qur'an speaks of the House and, in 2:127, Abraham and Ishmael raising its foundations. This is **a contrast/loose motif**, not a biblical attestation of the Kaaba or of an Abraham–Ishmael construction episode.

## Research limitations
The Hebrew Bible's literary composition and redaction, manuscript transmission, and modern translation dates must be distinguished. Sefaria's current displayed Masoretic witness and modern English rendering do not establish what versions were circulating in seventh-century Arabia. More investigation is required into Jewish, Christian, Syriac, and other relevant pre-Islamic traditions; later narratives cannot be backdated without evidence. Other verses 2:121–123, 126, 128–130 are screened preliminarily; no specific source established does **not** mean none exists.
