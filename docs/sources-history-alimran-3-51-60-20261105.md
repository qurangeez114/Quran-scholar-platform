# Jesus, the disciples, divine vindication, and Adam — Āl ʿImrān 3:51–60

**Research status:** unpublished preliminary historical dossier, 2026-11-05. This is not a public Sources card or a comprehensive textual-critical edition review.

## Historical and literary setting
This passage moves from Jesus' monotheistic proclamation (3:51), to disciples' assistance and testimony (3:52–53), a plot and God's response (3:54–55), judgment (3:56–58), and the analogy of Jesus to Adam (3:59–60). The unit is not one undifferentiated Gospel parallel. Different verses require different evidence and several have no specific earlier source identified in this screening.

## Chronology and textual transmission
Genesis is an ancient Hebrew-scriptural composition with complex formation and transmission, not a single securely dated manuscript. The Greek Gospel of Luke is usually placed in the late first century CE, while the surviving witnesses are later. The ESV and NABRE/USCCB pages linked above are modern English editions, **not** evidence for the date of the underlying composition. A future publication dossier must specify the Hebrew/Greek critical editions and witness readings used, and separately document any relevant Syriac reception.

## Interpretation and limitations
The Qur'anic disciples' declaration should not be mechanically equated with every Gospel discipleship scene. For 3:55, Muslim exegetical interpretations of *tawaffī* and Christian resurrection/ascension accounts require distinct treatment, not a simplistic equivalence. For 3:59, a comparison with [1 Corinthians 15:45–49](https://bible.usccb.org/bible/1corinthians/15) may illuminate an additional Adam/Christ typology, but Pauline and Qur'anic arguments differ and that text has not been checked in this batch. Similarity does not establish literary borrowing or a direct transmission path.
