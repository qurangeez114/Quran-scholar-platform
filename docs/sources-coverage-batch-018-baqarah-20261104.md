## Primary-text comparisons
1. **2:163 ↔ Deuteronomy 6:4** ([Hebrew and translations](https://www.sefaria.org/Deuteronomy.6.4)). Both affirm one God, but the Shema directly addresses Israel and has debated translation nuance (“one” / “alone”); Qur'an 2:163 adds mercy epithets. Shared monotheistic doctrine, not proof of borrowing.
2. **2:165 ↔ Deuteronomy 6:5** ([Hebrew and translation](https://www.sefaria.org/Deuteronomy.6.5)). The Hebrew command to love God wholeheartedly differs from the Qur'anic rebuke of love directed to rivals and its eschatological warning. This is a loose ethical motif, not an identical episode.

## Transmission cautions
The underlying Deuteronomy literary traditions precede Islam; this does **not** establish the date of a particular textual recension, surviving manuscript witness, or the precise oral/written channel accessible in seventh-century Arabia. Modern online Hebrew Masoretic and English translations are working editions. Compare Samaritan Pentateuch and Qumran witnesses where materially relevant before any stronger claim.
