## Primary textual comparison candidate

**Qur'an 2:7 ↔ Isaiah 6:9–10**: both passages invoke heart, ears and eyes in an account of people unable to perceive or respond to a divine message. The Qur'an describes Allah as having set a seal upon hearts and hearing, with a covering over sight. Isaiah presents a commission to the prophet concerning a people's hearing, eyes and comprehension. These are related literary/theological motifs, with **different immediate contexts and verbal imagery**, not identical quotations or evidence of copying.

- Hebrew Bible source: [Isaiah 6:9–10, Sefaria Hebrew and 2023 JPS English](https://www.sefaria.org/Isaiah.6.9).
- Qur'an reference: [2:7, Quranic Arabic Corpus translation comparison](https://corpus.quran.com/translation.jsp?chapter=2&verse=7).
- Status: **candidate**, source-language critical analysis, historical redaction/edition history and interpretations still to investigate.
