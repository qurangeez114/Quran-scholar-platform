Student rights and responsibilities

  • Access to a syllabus for every course before enrollment (catalog).
  • Clear grading criteria and timely feedback (target turnaround: To be provided by QuranHikma).
  • Privacy of learning records (privacy policy).
  • Fair process for integrity concerns and grade appeals (integrity, appeals).
  • Reasonable accommodations (accessibility).

Students are responsible for honest work, respectful discussion, meeting deadlines and keeping account details private.

Academic policies

All courses follow the academic policies: integrity, grading, attendance, accessibility, privacy, terms and refunds.

Student support

  • Technical help: To be provided by QuranHikma.
  • Academic advising: To be provided by QuranHikma.
  • Instructor contact and office hours: listed in each syllabus and on the faculty directory.
  • Library and research tools: the QuranHikma research, tafsir comparison and hadith pages.

Academic records

Each student can view enrolled courses and progress on My Progress, and completed courses and certificates on Transcript & Certificates. Certificates are completion records. Whether they carry academic credit: To be provided by QuranHikma.

Frequently asked questions

Is QuranHikma accredited?

To be provided by QuranHikma. QuranHikma makes no accreditation claim unless it is stated here.

Are courses free?

Published course prices appear on each syllabus. Current tuition: To be provided by QuranHikma.

Do I get a certificate?

Students who complete all required lessons and assessments with at least the minimum grade receive a certificate of completion, viewable on the transcript page.

Can I retake a quiz?

Retake rules are set in each syllabus; see the grading policy.

Who teaches the courses?

See the faculty directory. Credentials shown there are marked as reviewed only after administrative verification.

My confirmation email didn't arrive.

Check your spam folder, then try signing up again or contact To be provided by QuranHikma.
