Prophetic Lifetime Campaigns

Each campaign is documented with Qurʾān, hadith and sīrah sources. Classification labels show where disputes exist — no certainty is imposed where evidence is mixed.

Why source counts differ: this page currently documents 28 personally-led and 65 commissioned expeditions (93 records). Some reconstructions report 73 commissioned expeditions (101 total); those additional eight are not presented here until their identification and sources are verified.
