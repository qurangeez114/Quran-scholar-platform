Comparison & evaluation framework

  • Source integrity
    Exact edition, volume, page, and verification.
  • Historical context
    Audience, period, occasion, and assumptions.
  • Textual evidence
    Direct citation versus later association.
  • Reasoning
    Legal method, inference, and counterarguments.
  • Disagreement
    Distinct scholarly positions, dated and attributed.
  • Impact
    Subsequent application and documented consequences.

Evaluations are research prompts, not automatic verdicts. Missing evidence must remain visibly unverified.
