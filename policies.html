Academic integrity

QuranHikma expects every student to do their own work honestly and to credit the work of others. Honest scholarship is central to the study of the Qur'an, hadith and Islamic history.

Violations include

  • Plagiarism: presenting another person's words, ideas, translations or data as one's own without citation.
  • Cheating: using unauthorized help, materials or people during a quiz or assignment, or sharing answers.
  • Fabrication: inventing or falsifying sources, citations, quotations, hadith references or data.
  • Unauthorized AI use: submitting AI-generated text or answers where the syllabus does not allow it, or without disclosure where disclosure is required.
  • Impersonation: having someone else complete coursework or assessments, or sharing account access.
  • Facilitation: knowingly helping another student commit any of the above.

Citation expectations

Qur'anic verses are cited by sura and verse (e.g. Q 2:255) with the translation named. Hadith are cited by collection, book and number, with the grading source where relevant. Books and articles are cited in full (author, title, publisher, year, pages). Each syllabus may name a required citation style: To be provided by QuranHikma.

Process

  1. The instructor documents the concern and informs the student in writing.
  2. The student may respond within 7 days.
  3. The instructor may resolve a first, minor case (e.g. a resubmission or a zero on the assignment) and reports it to To be provided by QuranHikma (academic office).
  4. Serious or repeated cases are reviewed by To be provided by QuranHikma (integrity panel), which may impose course failure, suspension or dismissal.
  5. The student may appeal the outcome in writing within 14 days.

Records of violations are kept confidential and used only for academic administration.

Incompletes

A student who has completed most of the course but cannot finish for documented, serious reasons may request an Incomplete from the instructor before the end of the term. Deadline to resolve an Incomplete: To be provided by QuranHikma.

Grade appeals

A student who believes a grade was calculated in error or applied inconsistently with the syllabus should (1) contact the instructor in writing within 14 days of the grade being posted; (2) if unresolved, submit a written appeal to To be provided by QuranHikma (academic office), who will respond in writing. Disagreement with an instructor's academic judgement alone is not grounds for appeal.

Automated quizzes

Auto-graded quizzes record the student's score at submission. Where a quiz permits retakes, the syllabus states how attempts count (e.g. highest or most recent).

Attendance and participation

Courses are delivered online and largely self-paced unless the syllabus lists live sessions. “Attendance” means regular, documented academic engagement, not simply logging in.

  • Students are expected to engage with course material each week the course is in session: completing lessons, quizzes, assignments or discussion posts.
  • Live sessions (where offered) are recorded when possible; students who miss one should review the recording and complete any related activity.
  • Discussion participation is assessed on substance, use of evidence and respectful engagement, as described in the syllabus.
  • A student with no academic activity for To be provided by QuranHikma consecutive days may be contacted by the instructor and may be administratively withdrawn after notice.
  • Absences for illness, emergencies or religious observance are accommodated; contact the instructor as early as possible.

Accessibility statement

QuranHikma aims for its website and courses to be usable by everyone, including people with disabilities. Our target is conformance with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA. We have not yet completed a formal accessibility audit, and some pages may not yet meet this target.

What we do

  • Provide text alternatives and captions or transcripts for course video where available.
  • Support keyboard navigation, readable contrast and resizable text.
  • Mark right-to-left Arabic text correctly so screen readers can read it.

Requesting accommodations

Students who need an academic accommodation (e.g. extended quiz time, alternative formats) should contact To be provided by QuranHikma (accessibility contact). Requests are handled confidentially, and documentation requirements are kept to what is reasonably needed.

Reporting a barrier

If you find content you cannot access, tell us at To be provided by QuranHikma with the page address. We aim to respond within To be provided by QuranHikma business days.

Privacy policy

This draft describes the kinds of information the QuranHikma website currently processes, based on how the site is built. It must be reviewed by the institution (and, where appropriate, legal counsel) before adoption.

Information we collect

  • Account details: email address and profile information you provide.
  • Learning records: enrollments, lesson progress, quiz scores, assignments and discussion posts.
  • Usage data: pages viewed and site interactions, used to improve the site.
  • Payments and donations are processed by third-party providers (such as PayPal); QuranHikma does not store full card numbers.

Service providers

The site uses Supabase for accounts and data storage and Netlify for hosting. Full list of processors and data locations: To be provided by QuranHikma.

Student records

Student learning records are visible to the student, their instructors and authorized administrators only, and are not sold. Students may request a copy or correction of their records at To be provided by QuranHikma. Record retention period: To be provided by QuranHikma.

Your choices

You may request deletion of your account and personal data at To be provided by QuranHikma, subject to records the institution must keep.

Terms of use

  • Users must provide accurate account information and keep their login private; accounts may not be shared.
  • Course materials are for the enrolled student's personal study. Redistribution requires permission: To be provided by QuranHikma (copyright owner and licence).
  • Discussion posts must be respectful, on topic and free of harassment; moderators may remove posts that break these rules.
  • Research and educational content is provided for learning and is not a religious ruling (fatwa) unless explicitly labelled with its source.
  • Governing law and dispute process: To be provided by QuranHikma.
