Instructor Guide

1. Accept your invitation

  1. A QuranHikma administrator authorizes you. You receive an email titled with an Accept invitation button.
  2. Click Accept invitation first. It confirms your email address. Logging in before this fails with “Your email address is not confirmed yet”.
  3. The link works once. If it has expired, ask the administrator to press Resend invitation email on your row, then open the newest email right away.

2. Set your password

  1. The invitation opens the welcome page. Under Choose your password, enter a password of at least 12 characters twice and press Save password and continue.
  2. If you were given a temporary password instead, log in at the account page. The Instructor Dashboard then shows Choose your own password: enter the Current temporary password, a New password (12–128 characters, different from the temporary one) and Confirm new password, then press Save new password.
  3. Forgot your password? On the account page choose Forgot password, enter your email, open the newest reset email, and choose a new password on the Choose a new password form.

3. Create a course

  1. On the Instructor Dashboard, choose Manage Courses.
  2. Under Create New Course, fill in Course Title and Description.
  3. Choose Student access: Private — only my enrolled students (default) or Public — students may discover and request access.
  4. Optionally tick Allow other instructors to include my course in their courses. This does not share your grades or authorship, and can be revoked.
  5. Press Create Course. The course appears under Your Courses as a Draft; students cannot see drafts.

4. Add lessons

  1. On the course card, press Lessons.
  2. Under Create New Lesson, enter a Lesson Title and choose a Lesson Type:
    • Text Content — write the reading in Lesson content / instructions.
    • Video Lesson — paste an HTTPS YouTube video URL and an optional summary.
    • Quiz — after saving, the Quiz Builder opens (see step 5).
    • Assignment — write what students must submit in Lesson content / instructions; these become the assignment instructions.
  3. Optionally add a Reflection question for students, then press Save Lesson.
  4. To add several lessons at once, list one title per line and press Create lessons. Each lesson can then be opened with Edit, copied with Duplicate or removed with Delete.

5. Build a quiz

  1. For a Quiz lesson, press Edit questions in the lesson list (it opens automatically after you save a new Quiz lesson).
  2. For each question fill in Question text, Choice A, Choice B and Choice C, select the Correct answer, and write the Explanation of the correct answer. Optionally explain Why this choice is wrong.
  3. Optionally open Optional reworded final check to add a reworded version; students then get a practice round with explanations before the graded final check.
  4. Use ＋ Add question for more questions and press Save quiz questions. The pass mark is fixed at 90%; the page shows how many correct answers that requires.
  5. Students see your explanations after they submit.

6. Preview as a student

  1. On the course card press Preview as student (or Preview lesson as student in the Quiz Builder).
  2. The preview shows the outline and a list of anything still missing before publishing. In lessons you can practice the quiz and see the explanations; nothing is saved (“Preview: progress is not saved”).

7. Publish

  1. On the course card press Publish.
  2. If something is missing, a Before publishing checklist shows what is done (✓) and what is still missing (✗): at least one lesson; every lesson complete (reading text, an HTTPS video link, quiz questions with a 90% pass mark, assignment instructions); and, once the academic catalog is enabled, a syllabus with learning outcomes and a weekly schedule.
  3. The syllabus is entered by a QuranHikma administrator in the Syllabus & Outcomes editor. If that item is missing, ask the administrator to complete it.
  4. To hide a course again, press Unpublish.

8. Enroll students

  1. On the Instructor Dashboard, use Enroll a student in your course: enter Name and Email, choose the course under Enroll in course, and press Enroll student.
  2. A student without a QuranHikma account also needs a Temporary password (new accounts only) of at least 12 characters. Give it to the student separately; it is never emailed. The student must click the invitation email first, then replace the temporary password.
  3. For several existing students at once, open Bulk Enrollment, choose one of My courses, paste one email per line (or upload a CSV) and press Start Enrollment. Choosing another instructor’s published course sends a recommendation that the student and that instructor must approve.
  4. If the email did not arrive, use Resend on the student’s row.

9. Grade work

  1. Quizzes are graded automatically. See them under Results on the course card, or the ✅ Quizzes (Auto-Graded) tab of the Grading Dashboard.
  2. For assignments, open the Grading Dashboard, choose a submission under Pending Submissions, fill in the rubric scores, the Grade (0–100) and Feedback, and press Submit Grade.

If something goes wrong

  • “Your email address is not confirmed yet” — open the invitation email and click its button, or choose Resend confirmation email on the account page.
  • The link “has expired or was already used” — ask the administrator to resend it and open only the newest email.
  • Publish is blocked — follow the Before publishing checklist.
