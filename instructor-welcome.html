How to activate your instructor account

  1. Open the invitation link from your registered email. This confirms your address and opens your instructor setup.
  2. If the administrator gave you a temporary password, use it to sign in. The password is delivered separately from your invitation email.
  3. Open your Instructor Dashboard and choose a new password of at least 12 characters before creating classes.
  4. You can also use Forgot password to receive a secure password-reset email and set a new password instead.
