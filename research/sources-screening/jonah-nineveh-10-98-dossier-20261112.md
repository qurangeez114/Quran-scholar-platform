# Jonah / Nineveh: preliminary historical dossier (not public)

**Qur'an:** 10:98. **Earlier source:** Hebrew Bible, Jonah 3:5–10. **Status:** staged comparison only; not a verified/public association.

## Primary texts inspected
- Qur'an 10:98 Arabic, https://quran.com/10/98 (also https://www.sultan.org/quran/surah/10/).
- Jonah 3:5–10, Hebrew text and Revised JPS 2023 English translation, https://www.sefaria.org/Jonah.3.5-10 . This modern translation is not the date of the ancient work.

## Comparison
Both texts describe a community associated with Jonah/Yunus believing and the threatened punishment being averted. Jonah 3:5–10 specifies Nineveh, public fasting, sackcloth, royal response, abandonment of evil and divine relenting. Qur'an 10:98 instead identifies the people of Yunus and stresses their exceptional beneficial faith and temporary worldly reprieve, without these narrative particulars. The shared narrative is a strong episode-level parallel, not proof of direct literary dependence.

## Chronology, witnesses, limits
The Book of Jonah belongs to the pre-Islamic Hebrew Bible, but its literary composition date must be distinguished from the dates of extant manuscripts. No manuscript collation, Dead Sea Scrolls coverage check, Septuagint comparison, or textual-critical edition audit has yet been completed for this dossier. No specific transmission route into the Qur'anic milieu is established. The Revised JPS translation consulted is dated 2023, not a pre-Islamic witness. Do not publish a verified association before source and witness provenance review.
