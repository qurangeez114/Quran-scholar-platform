# Exclusive worship: Qur'an 10:104 and Deuteronomy 6:13–14 (unpublished research note)

**Status:** preliminary, staged, not a verified public source association. **Qur'an:** [10:104](https://quran.com/10/104). **Earlier passage:** [Deuteronomy 6:13–14](https://www.sefaria.org/Deuteronomy.6.13-14).

## Text and relationship
The Qur'anic speaker declares that he does not worship what others worship apart from Allah and instead worships the God who causes death. Deuteronomy commands Israel to revere and serve YHWH, not follow other gods of surrounding peoples. Both oppose worship of other deities. Deuteronomy is covenantal instruction to Israel, and includes oaths; Qur'an 10:104 is a prophetic declaration to a broader audience, emphasizing death and belief. This is a **shared doctrinal motif**, not an episode parallel or demonstrated literary dependence. Qur'an 10:105 continues the exhortation toward *hanif* religion, but is not assigned a second independent source relationship here.

## Source and dating caution
The Hebrew passage and English translation were inspected through Sefaria's Deuteronomy chapter display. The Hebrew Bible is a pre-Islamic textual tradition, but the precise literary composition, textual layers, extant witness dates, and relation of Masoretic, Samaritan and Greek witnesses have **not** been audited for these verses. The modern English translation is not a pre-Islamic witness. No specific route of textual transmission into the Qur'anic milieu has been established. No public database record should be created on this evidence alone.
