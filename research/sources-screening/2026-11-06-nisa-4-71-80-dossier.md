# An-Nisāʾ 4:71–80 — unpublished preliminary history dossier

Date: 2026-11-06. Batch 058. **Research-only; not a public verified dossier.**

## Passage context
The sequence moves from military precaution and hesitant participants (4:71–74) to advocacy for vulnerable people (4:75), contrasting causes of fighting (4:76), a dispute over timing of combat obligations (4:77), mortality and attribution of events (4:78–79), and prophetic authority (4:80). The discourse is not a single historical episode that can be mapped mechanically onto one biblical narrative.
