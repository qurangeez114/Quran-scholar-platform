# Yunus 10:107 and Deuteronomy 32:39 — unpublished comparison
Reviewed 2026-11-12. **Status: staged, not a verified/public Sources association.**

## Text and relationship
Qur'an [10:107](https://www.rasulullah.org/en/quran/10) says that if God touches a person with adversity, no one but God removes it, and if God intends good, none can repel the bounty. [Deuteronomy 32:39](https://www.sefaria.org/Deuteronomy.32.39) (Hebrew text; compare [Sefaria transcription and English rendering](https://voices.sefaria.org/sheets/650648)) declares divine control of death, life, wounding and healing, and no deliverer from God's hand. **Classification:** broad divine-sovereignty motif, not a distinctive phrase-for-phrase parallel.

## Important differences
Deuteronomy is embedded in the Song of Moses, an address to Israel invoking divine judgment and deliverance. Qur'an 10:107 is an address about individual adversity and beneficence, concluding with forgiveness and mercy. Deuteronomy explicitly mentions death/life and wound/heal; the Qur'anic verse does not. The overlap cannot establish textual borrowing, common intermediary, or historical contact.

## Chronology and textual witnesses
Deuteronomy is a pre-Islamic biblical work, but the composition and redaction history of the Song of Moses are debated; the dates of surviving Hebrew manuscripts are not identical to dates of composition. No critical-edition apparatus, Dead Sea Scrolls witnesses, Septuagint variant, Syriac Peshitta reading or direct transmission history was checked in this pass. The Hebrew and English displayed by Sefaria are online reading texts, not a substitute for textual criticism.
