# An-Nisāʾ 4:31–40 — unpublished comparative dossier (first pass)

**Status:** Research staging only; not a published History dossier. Reviewed 2026-11-06. No literary dependence claimed.

## Passage structure
4:31–33 concerns prohibited acts, economic entitlements, and inheritance/covenants; 4:34–35 addresses marital conflict and family arbitration; 4:36–39 addresses obligations to other people, generosity, concealment and public display; 4:40 describes divine justice. Do not collapse these into a single borrowed narrative.
