# Āl ʿImrān 3:191–200: unpublished comparative research dossier

**Research status:** preliminary; 2026-11-05. Not a public verified dossier. No historical dependence claim.

## Passage and internal chronology

3:191–194 contains remembrance of God, reflection on creation, and prayer for salvation and fulfillment of promises. 3:195 answers the petition, mentioning both sexes and experiences of migration and persecution in the Qur'anic community. 3:196–198 contrasts short-lived worldly success with final recompense; 3:199 discusses faithful among the People of Scripture; 3:200 exhorts perseverance. These are not a single earlier historical story, and the segment should not be assigned an invented common source.

## Text and dating caveats

The Psalter preserves a pre-Islamic Hebrew scriptural tradition; a precise composition date for Psalm 19 should not be asserted without scholarship on the psalm's literary history. Composition, textual transmission and the dates of surviving Hebrew/Greek manuscripts are distinct questions. Psalm numbering differs in Greek/Latin traditions. The English rendering accessed through a Sefaria source sheet is not a substitute for a critical Hebrew edition or witness collation. Neither a shared creation motif nor religious proximity demonstrates literary borrowing.

## Alternative interpretations and outstanding research

The resemblance may reflect a widely shared monotheistic contemplation-of-creation tradition or independent development of religious imagery. More focused investigation could compare 3:193–194 with pre-Islamic penitential prayers, but no candidate has been screened to publication standard here. The passages concerning migration, believers of the Book and final reward require contextual analysis rather than automatic motif matching.
