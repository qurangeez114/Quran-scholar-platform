# Āl ʿImrān 3:171–180 — unpublished research dossier

Status: preliminary screening only, 2026-11-05. No new verified primary-text relationships. Not for public Sources cards.

## Context and per-verse coverage
3:171 continues the martyrs' rejoicing theme of 3:169–170; do not duplicate or promote the earlier Wisdom of Solomon / 2 Maccabees leads without critical collation. 3:172–175 concern post-conflict perseverance, threats and trust in God. 3:176–178 discuss disbelief and divine respite. 3:179 addresses differentiation of the wicked and good. 3:180 warns against withholding bounty and future accountability.

## Dating, witnesses, transmission
The Gospel of Matthew and Malachi represent pre-Islamic textual traditions, but work composition dates must not be confused with dates of extant Greek/Hebrew manuscripts. No original-language critical editions, textual variants or surviving witness dates have been collated for this batch. The English/online locators are research navigation aids, not certification of a verified passage. No inference of direct literary dependence is warranted.

## Interpretive alternatives
The comparisons may reflect broad shared monotheistic themes rather than identifiable textual transmission. In 3:171–178, generic themes are insufficient to establish specific pre-Islamic verse parallels; keep classification as needing further research.
