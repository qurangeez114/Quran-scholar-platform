## Chronology and transmission limits
The biblical Flood account is an earlier textual tradition than the Qur'an, but its literary formation/redaction is debated. Do not substitute the date of a surviving Hebrew, Greek, or Syriac manuscript for the date of composition. The Qur'an belongs to seventh-century Arabian religious discourse; the exact dating of a particular Qur'anic manuscript witness is a separate issue. No Septuagint, Peshitta, Qumran, midrashic or other extra-biblical witnesses were collated in this pass. No dependence claim is justified.
