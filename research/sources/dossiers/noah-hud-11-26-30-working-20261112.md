# Noah's warning and the rejection of his followers — research dossier (unpublished)

**Scope:** Qur'an 11:25–31, with this screening covering 11:26–30. **Status:** preliminary; not a public Sources/History dossier. Reviewed 2026-11-12.

## Texts and chronological context
- Qur'an 11:26–30, Arabic and Sahih International English, https://legacy.quran.com/11/26-44 (7th-century Qur'anic discourse; precise verse witness history not evaluated here).
- Hebrew Genesis 6:5–9, 6:13–22, 7:1–5, Hebrew and English as displayed by Sefaria, https://www.sefaria.org/Genesis.6-9. The Genesis narrative is part of an earlier Hebrew scriptural tradition; dates of composition, redaction and individual surviving manuscripts must not be conflated and require separate critical bibliography.

## Comparison
| Qur'an | Earlier passage | Relationship | Important difference |
|---|---|---|---|
| 11:26 | Genesis 6:5–7 | broad shared flood-judgment setting | Qur'an puts an exclusive-worship warning in Noah's mouth; Genesis reports divine observation and decision, not this speech |
| 11:27 | Genesis 6:9–22 | narrative contrast only | Genesis does not report elites ridiculing Noah's low-status adherents |
| 11:28 | Genesis 6–9 | no specific parallel established in checked passage | The Qur'anic argument about mercy and compulsion is not the Genesis dialogue |
| 11:29 | Genesis 6–9 | no direct parallel in checked passage | No demand for payment or expulsion of believers appears in Genesis |
| 11:30 | Genesis 6–9 | no direct parallel in checked passage | No matching rhetorical question about expelling believers |

**Interpretation:** The common figure Noah and impending punishment warrant an episode-level comparison, not five manufactured verse-specific biblical citations. The Qur'an gives Noah an extended public disputation; Genesis focuses on divine judgment, Noah's righteousness, instructions and the ark. Similarities alone do not prove literary dependence.

## Transmission and unresolved evidence
The Hebrew displayed in Sefaria is a textual access point, not proof of having collated Biblia Hebraica Quinta, Dead Sea Scroll witnesses, Septuagint variants, Syriac Peshitta or Samaritan witnesses. Manuscript dates must be reported separately from hypothesized composition dates. Later rabbinic expansions (including Genesis Rabbah) and Christian/Syriac Noah preaching traditions should be searched for exact source passages and pre-Islamic dating; no specific uninspected passage is asserted here. No Zoroastrian correspondence is claimed.
