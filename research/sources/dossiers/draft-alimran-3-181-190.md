# Āl ʿImrān 3:181–190 — draft historical dossier (not public)

**Status:** Preliminary research, 2026-11-05. No primary-language critical edition or manuscript collation performed. No verified public source relationship.

## Context and chronology

Qur'an 3:181–184 addresses accusations about God, killing of prophets, fire-consuming sacrifices, and rejection of messengers. 3:185–190 turns to death, trials, scriptural responsibility, divine sovereignty and natural signs. The Hebrew Bible contains traditions formed before Islam, but date of composition, textual redaction and date of surviving manuscripts must not be conflated. The comparison is between texts, not evidence that the Qur'an copied an identifiable written exemplar.

## Historical interpretation and limitations

The first comparison concerns a shared charge of violence toward prophets. The other two concern specific sacrificial fire narratives. These are not proof of direct literary dependence. Alternative explanations include a shared scriptural discourse and later oral retellings; neither can be established from resemblance alone. The Qur'anic polemic must not be represented as an objective claim about all Jews or Christians. The reported speaker and audience are situated in the Qur'anic rhetoric and require contextual interpretation.
