## Historical background and verse mapping

The sequence 3:151–155 addresses the aftermath of battle, commonly situated in Islamic exegetical tradition around Uhud: fear, a setback after initial success, retreat, distress, sleep, and pardon. Such events are not pre-Islamic and must not be conflated with older military narratives. Verses 3:156–158 address death and divine reward; 3:159 emphasizes gentleness, forgiveness, consultation and reliance upon God; 3:160 invokes divine assistance.
